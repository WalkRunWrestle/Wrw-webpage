Support Desk

Got questions? We have answers.

Straightforward info with zero fluff. Need help? We’re always here for you.

Can I walk the challenges?

100% yes! Walking, jogging, running, or a mix of all three is completely welcome.

Do I have to complete it in one go?

Not unless the specific challenge rules say so! Each event specifies whether it's flexible, daily, or streak-based.

How do I log my distance?

Use the web-based WRW Hub to record your sessions and keep track of your active challenges.

How does proof verification work?

Simply upload a screenshot from your smartwatch, treadmill, or tracking app into the Hub once completed.

When does my medal ship?

Hardware is dispatched once your challenge is complete and your proof is verified by our team.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! UK delivery is included, and worldwide shipping is available at checkout.
