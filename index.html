Support Desk

Got questions? We have answers.

Straightforward info with zero fluff. If something has gone weird, tell us. We have probably tested it first.

Can I walk the challenges?

100% yes! Walking, jogging, running, or a mix of all three is completely welcome.

Do I have to complete it in one go?

Not unless the specific challenge rules say so. Each event explains whether it is flexible, daily, or streak-based.

How do I log my distance?

Use the web-based WRW Hub to record your sessions and keep track of your active challenge.

How does proof verification work?

When an event requires proof, upload a screenshot from your smartwatch, treadmill, or tracking app through the Hub.

When will I receive my medal?

Check the individual event page and your order/completion information for the current medal fulfilment details.

Where do you ship?

Current delivery options, costs and destinations are shown during checkout. If you are unsure, contact us before ordering.
